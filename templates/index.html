🚀 About AI Career Roadmap Planner

AI Career Roadmap Planner is an intelligent career guidance platform designed to help students plan their journey in Artificial Intelligence.

The system analyzes user goals, education level, programming experience, and interests to generate a personalized learning roadmap. Users can track completed skills, analyze progress, and receive AI-based career recommendations.
